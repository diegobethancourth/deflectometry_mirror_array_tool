How it works

Model assumptions. Slope terms are treated as independent and combined in quadrature. Each one is then integrated into height by its spatial character, not by a single rule: random error (phase noise) averages down as a random walk, δs·√(L·Δx); figure error (screen planarity) is correlated across the aperture so it integrates coherently, δs·L; a tilt-like term (a uniform screen-pose offset) gives every point the same slope error and is therefore removed exactly by the reconstruction's piston-and-tilt fit; a scale term (distance uncertainty) reproduces the sag scaled by δd/d. Systematic inputs are entered as 1σ equivalents. Slope, not height, is what deflectometry measures directly — treat §6 as the primary result and the height column as what it costs you if the screen geometry is only known this well.
