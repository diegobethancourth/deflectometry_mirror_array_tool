How it works
Every mirror sits at a different place in the array, so each one needs its own tilt to fold the camera's line of sight onto the screen. This solves the law of reflection per mirror — n̂ ∝ v̂ − û — converts the result into piezo displacement Δ = L·tanθ, and checks it against the mount's tilt limit and the actuator's travel and step size.
